Every image is licensed **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) by the holding museum, as stated in the record's Europeana rights field.
They are reproduced here, resized and arranged into a figure (an adaptation), for non-commercial academic research. The adapted figure is shared under the same licence, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
All other figures in this repository contain no third-party images. Raw images are never committed (`data/` is git-ignored).
